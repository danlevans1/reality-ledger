# 2026 Oregon Voter Guide — Editorial & Compliance Guardrails

Status: publication draft  
Research date: 2026-10-06  
Jurisdiction: Oregon; federal, state, Crook County and City of Prineville races

## Editorial purpose

The voter guide is a factual, source-transparent publication. It does not endorse, oppose, rank, score or recommend candidates.

Each candidate in a covered race is presented using comparable categories. Missing information is labeled as a research gap rather than inferred from party affiliation.

## Source hierarchy

1. Official election records, ballots and voters' pamphlets.
2. Official legislative, congressional and government records.
3. Candidate-controlled 2026 websites or statements, explicitly treated as candidate claims.
4. Reputable independent journalism for context and interviews.
5. Advocacy-group material only for clearly attributed facts such as an endorsement or questionnaire response.

## Required language rules

- Say "the candidate says," "the campaign states," or equivalent when a claim comes from campaign material and has not been independently verified.
- Do not describe a candidate as "biblical," "unbiblical," "best," "worst," "most aligned," or assign values scores.
- Do not infer abortion, marriage, immigration or other positions solely from party affiliation.
- Do not imply that a nonresponse to a questionnaire proves a position.
- Do not reproduce long passages from candidate or government publications; summarize and link.
- Correct factual errors transparently and preserve the correction history.

## Election-law guardrails

This publication should remain independent of candidates and campaigns. Do not give campaigns editorial control, share pre-publication drafts for approval, accept nonpublic strategy information, or coordinate timing, wording, targeting or paid distribution with a candidate or political committee.

Oregon election law can treat spending on candidate communications within the statutory pre-election window as an independent expenditure in some circumstances, including communications naming clearly identified candidates. Oregon also contains an exclusion for qualifying nonpartisan voters' guides. Before paid promotion or significant dedicated spending, obtain current guidance from the Oregon Elections Division or qualified Oregon election counsel regarding registration, reporting and disclaimer obligations.

For federal races, ordinary unpaid individual internet activity and bona fide editorial activity receive different treatment from paid political advertising. Paid placement on third-party platforms can trigger federal public-communication and disclaimer rules.

## Paid promotion

Do not buy ads, boosted posts or sponsored distribution for this guide until Oregon and federal campaign-finance treatment has been checked for the exact proposed spend and placement.

## Candidate corrections

Candidates and readers may submit primary-source corrections through the public issue tracker. Corrections do not give campaigns editorial control.
