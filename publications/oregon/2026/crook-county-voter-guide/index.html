Prineville & Crook County Voter Guide

General Election: November 3, 2026. This guide summarizes documented candidate positions, experience and public records for races relevant to many Crook County voters. It does not endorse, rank or recommend candidates.

How to read this guide. Candidate campaign statements are labeled as such and are not treated as independently verified facts. Official government records are preferred for voting history and officeholder background. If a position could not be verified from current 2026 material, the guide says so instead of inferring it from party affiliation.

Last researched: October 6, 2026. The official Oregon voters’ pamphlet notes that candidate statements are printed as submitted and are not fact-checked by the Secretary of State. Your individual ballot is the final authority for which races apply to you.

Method and corrections

Reality Ledger uses the same editorial rules for every candidate in a race: comparable issue categories, preference for primary sources, explicit labeling of campaign claims, and no inference from party affiliation when candidate-specific evidence is missing.

The official Oregon voters’ pamphlet is a core source for ballot status and candidate-submitted statements. The Secretary of State says those statements are printed as submitted and are not fact-checked. This guide therefore treats them as statements by the candidate, not independent verification.

Corrections supported by a primary source can be submitted through the Reality Ledger public issue tracker. Corrections should be recorded transparently rather than silently changing the historical record.
